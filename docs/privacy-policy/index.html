Privacy Policy for Telecalling

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Telecalling is an internal customer-relationship-management (CRM) app built by ans (the "Company") for our own telecalling agents to manage and call sales leads. The app is distributed only to authorized Company employees/agents and is not intended for use by the general public.

Information We Collect

To operate the app, we collect and store:

  • Agent account information: login ID, name, and encrypted credentials, issued and managed by a supervisor — agents do not self-register.
  • Lead/customer information entered or accessed by agents in the course of their work: name, phone number, address, PAN, date of birth, income, and employer/company details.
  • Device identifiers (such as device model or ID), used for app functionality and troubleshooting.
  • Presence of specific competing apps: the app checks whether a small, fixed list of named competing financial-referral apps (currently GroMo, Zet, and MyMoneyMantra) is installed on the device, and reports this to the Company. This check is limited to that named list only — it does not read, list, or report any other apps installed on the device.
  • Call duration: after an agent places a call to an assigned lead from the app, the app reads the duration recorded by the phone for that one call (using the Call Log permission) and reports only that duration, in seconds, to the Company. It does not read, store, or report any other call-log entries, phone numbers, or call history.
  • Call timing: from the moment an agent taps Call on an assigned lead until that call ends, the app observes the phone's call state (using the Phone permission) and reports only how many seconds the call lasted from dialling to hang-up. It does not record call audio, and does not read or report phone numbers or any other call activity.

How We Use Information

Collected data is used solely to operate the app's core functionality: enabling agents to view, manage, and call assigned leads, and to authenticate agents. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Data Sharing

We do not share collected data with third parties, other than infrastructure/service providers (such as our backend database host) who process it solely to provide the app's services on our behalf, under contractual confidentiality obligations.

Data Storage & Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections and stored in our secured backend database.

Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained for as long as necessary for business/CRM purposes. Agents or employees who wish to have their account data deleted should contact their supervisor or reach us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is used exclusively by adult Company employees.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
